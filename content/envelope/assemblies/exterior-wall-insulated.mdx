Target assembly R-value: **R-34.5** (U-0.029 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Primary air barrier to be [Adhero 3000](https://475.supply/products/adhero-3000) or sim. self-adhered
  vapor-permeable membrane on the outer face of the sheathing, behind the continuous insulation, as detailed on A001.
  Secondary interior air barrier of [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) with
  [Tescon Vana](https://475.supply/products/tescon-vana) at the seams wherever the gypsum is new.
- 5" of [Rockwool Comfortboard 80](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortboard/) continuous
  exterior insulation. Prefer two staggered 2½" layers over one 5" layer: the offset joints close the gaps that a
  single layer leaves at every board edge.
- 3½" of [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/) in the 2x4 stud
  cavity where the studs are exposed. Where the existing gypsum stays and cellulose is dense-packed from outside
  (A503), the cavity R-value runs a little lower, R-3.7 to R-3.8 per inch against R-4.3, and the assembly lands near
  R-33; the difference is small next to the exterior layer.
- Support the cladding on the 3/8" vertical battens with stainless-steel screws through the Comfortboard into the
  studs, or on low-conductivity clips. See the
  [Rockwool cladding attachment guide](https://www.rockwool.com/siteassets/o2-rockwool/documentation/technical-guides/commercial/cavityrock-and-comfortboard-cladding-attachment-solutions-technical-guide.pdf)
  for fastener spacing and screw sizing at 5".
- The windows are overframed to the exterior into the insulation layer (A502). This is the right place for them
  thermally; draw the buck, the sill pan and the tape sequence on A501 for every opening type, since the installed
  window U-value in the model depends on it.
- Sheets A301 and GN1 still carry an earlier wall specification (2" XPS, ZIP sheathing, ExoAir fluid-applied WRB,
  spray foam). Reconcile the set to the A001 assembly before bidding.
