Target assembly R-value: **R-49.8** (U-0.020 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Same build-up as the metal roof, under a T.P.O. membrane: [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus)
  air barrier at the underside of the joists, 9¼" of [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/)
  in the 2x10 cavity, 5/8" sheathing, a self-adhered membrane, and 5" of
  [Rockwool Comfortboard 80](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortboard/) in two staggered layers.
- The joist cavity is unvented; the same
  [R806.5](https://up.codes/viewer/connecticut/irc-2021/chapter/8/roof-ceiling-construction#R806.5) requirement for
  R-20 of rigid insulation above the deck applies here.
- A vented cavity works less well on a low-slope roof than on a pitched one, because there is little height
  difference to drive air through it. Detail an inlet and outlet at opposite edges, keep the cavity clear of
  blocking, and confirm the venting with the architect. If the cavity is dropped, the assembly becomes a
  conventional unvented compact roof and the membrane goes directly over the Comfortboard on a cover board.
- Detail the perimeter upstands, curbs and drains so the Comfortboard stays continuous to the edge and meets the
  wall insulation. Tie the roof membrane to the wall [Adhero 3000](https://475.supply/products/adhero-3000) plane at
  the parapet or fascia so the air barrier is continuous.
- Deck areas (TimberTech on sleepers over the T.P.O.) add no insulation; keep the sleepers off the membrane on
  pads, and confirm the extra dead load with the structural engineer.
