Target assembly R-value: **R-12.4** (U-0.081 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Note: all concrete sizing as per the Structural Engineer. Concrete is shown for illustration purposes only.
- 2½" of [Rockwool Comfortboard 80](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortboard/) on the outside
  face of the new foundation wall, over Tremco ExoAir 110 or sim. waterproofing, with a drainage mat with integral
  filter fabric outside the insulation, as detailed on A001 detail 5.
- Tie the [Adhero 3000](https://475.supply/products/adhero-3000) wall membrane to the waterproofing with
  [Contega Solido IQ](https://475.supply/products/contega-solido-iq) at the sill. This is the one place the
  above-grade air barrier meets the below-grade waterproofing, and it has to be drawn.
- Carry the Comfortboard up past grade to meet the 5" wall layer, with a cover board and hemmed metal flashing
  from the sheathing over its top. The step from 5" to 2½" at the transition is a small thermal bridge; keep it
  above grade where it can be seen and flashed.
- Take the insulation down to the footing and connect it to the sub-slab layer, so the slab edge is not a
  continuous bridge around the addition.
- Insect screen at the base of the vented cavity above, and parge or protect the exposed insulation between grade
  and the siding.
