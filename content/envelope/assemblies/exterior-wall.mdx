As drawn and modeled: **R-12.8** (U-0.078 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F). EnerPHit By Component comparison: **R-18**.

- This is the garage-side existing wall on A501: [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus), 2x4
  studs with 3½" of [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/),
  sheathing, [Adhero 3000](https://475.supply/products/adhero-3000), and siding with no Comfortboard and no
  rainscreen. It is the weakest assembly above grade.
- The As-Drawn package carries it as drawn. The component variant shows that raising it to R-18 is worth about
  600 kWh/yr of heating demand, a small number next to the crawlspace floor and the junctions.
- Confirm every location assigned to this wall type on the plans. Where the exterior layer can go, even 1½" of
  Comfortboard 80 brings the wall to about R-19 and removes the thermal bridge at every stud; where the garage
  slab or roof blocks it, leave the wall as drawn.
- Air sealing matters more than R-value at a garage wall. Seal the Adhero plane continuously to the garage
  ceiling membrane and to the foundation, and see the garage ceiling card for the indoor-air-quality reasons.
- Keep the wall vapor-open to the exterior. Without exterior insulation the sheathing runs cold in winter, so the
  interior membrane has to be the Intello variable-permeance product, not polyethylene.
