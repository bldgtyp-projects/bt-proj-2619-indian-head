Target assembly R-value: **R-50.5** (U-0.020 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Primary air barrier to be [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) or sim. variable-permeance
  membrane along the underside of the rafters, seams taped with [Tescon Vana](https://475.supply/products/tescon-vana),
  as detailed on A501 to A503. Carry it across the eave and rake to meet the wall membrane without a break.
- Rafter cavity (2x10, 9¼") filled with [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/)
  mineral-fiber batt, or a dense-packed cellulose equivalent where the cavity is filled from outside.
- 5" of [Rockwool Comfortboard 80](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortboard/) continuous above
  the roof deck, in two staggered 2½" layers with all joints offset. This layer is what wraps the rafters and lifts
  the roof to R-50.
- The rafter cavity is unvented, so the code's unvented-roof provisions apply
  ([2021 IRC R806.5 as adopted in Connecticut](https://up.codes/viewer/connecticut/irc-2021/chapter/8/roof-ceiling-construction#R806.5)):
  with air-permeable batt below the deck, rigid insulation of at least **R-20** is required directly above the deck
  in Climate Zone 5. Five inches of Comfortboard 80 is about R-21. Confirm with the architect and the building
  official that mineral-wool board satisfies the "rigid board" language, since that section was written around
  foam.
- The 2½" vented cavity above the Comfortboard, the second sheathing layer and the copper standing seam are outside
  the thermal boundary. Keep the vent cavity open at the eave and ridge with insect screen at both ends.
- Use the 2½" interior service cavity shown in the study for wiring and lighting. Recessed fixtures punched through
  the air barrier are the most common source of leakage at a roof.
- The drawing set names the ice-and-water layer differently on different sheets (VaporDry SA on A502/A503, Adhero
  3000 on A001/A501) and the interior membrane as MemBrain on A104. Resolve to one product per layer before pricing.
