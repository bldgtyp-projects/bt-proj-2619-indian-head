Target assembly R-value: **R-16.4** (U-0.061 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- 3½" of [Rockwool SmartRock](https://475.supply/products/smartrock) or sim. semi-rigid mineral fiber against the
  inside face of the existing concrete wall, with [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) inboard of
  it. Mineral fiber keeps the assembly vapor-open to the interior so the concrete can dry inward; foam does not.
- The drawing set (A501) shows 2" EPS at the existing basement walls plus batt in a stud cavity. Two inches of EPS
  is about R-8. Bring the drawing to the 3½" mineral-fiber layer studied here, or re-run the model at the lower
  value.
- Check the wall for bulk water and efflorescence before any insulation goes in, and fix drainage from outside
  where it is found. Interior insulation does not cure a wet wall; it hides it.
- Run the [Adhero 3000](https://475.supply/products/adhero-3000) wall air barrier down to the foundation and tape
  it to the concrete with [Contega Solido IQ](https://475.supply/products/contega-solido-iq), so the cellar wall,
  the rim joist and the wall above form one continuous line.
- Insulate the rim joist with the 5½" Comfortbatt shown on A502/A503 and air-seal each joist bay first. The rim is
  where interior cellar insulation usually fails.
- No 6-mil polyethylene against the concrete, whatever the GN1 boilerplate says.
