Target assembly R-value: **R-21.9** (U-0.046 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Note: all concrete sizing as per the Structural Engineer. Concrete is shown for illustration purposes only.
- 4" of sub-slab insulation at R-5 per inch wherever the cellar floor is being replaced:
  [HFO XPS](https://www.owenscorning.com/en-us/insulation/commercial/foamular-ngx),
  [Neopor graphite EPS](https://neopor-insulation.com/) or sim., in two 2" layers with board edges offset at least
  12" and gaps filled with low-expansion foam.
- The drawing set shows 2½" of Comfortboard 80 under the new slab (A001 detail 5, about R-10) and 2" of XPS in the
  GN1 boilerplate. Bring the drawing to 4" of either product, or accept the lower value and re-run the model.
- Sub-slab air and vapor barrier directly below the concrete and above the insulation:
  [15 mil StegoHome](https://475.supply/products/stegohome-15-mil-vapor-barrier) or sim., with every seam and
  penetration sealed with [StegoTape](https://475.supply/products/stego-tape) or
  [StegoMastic](https://475.supply/products/stego-mastic).
- Carry the insulation up the inside face of the foundation wall to meet the wall insulation, so the slab edge
  does not stay a continuous thermal bridge around the perimeter.
- Check the finished floor height against headroom, stair rise and door thresholds before the depth is fixed.
- The remaining crawlspace floor, which cannot be reached, stays in its existing condition and is outside this
  detail. Its cost is shown in the EnerPHit By Component column above.
