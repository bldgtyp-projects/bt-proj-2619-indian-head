Target assembly R-value: **R-31.7** (U-0.032 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- 9¼" of [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/) filling the 2x10
  joist cavity, in full contact with the subfloor above. Gaps between the batt and the subfloor are the usual reason
  a floor over a garage feels cold.
- Attached garages are a common source of poor indoor air quality (exhaust, fuel, solvents, paint). Every surface
  between the garage and the house needs its own air barrier. See the
  [U.S. DOE guide to air-sealing attached garages](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/air-sealing-attached-garage).
- Airtightness layer of [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) or sim. along the underside of the
  joists, on the garage side, taped to the garage-side wall membrane and to the rim joist. Install it before the
  fire-rated gypsum ceiling the code requires, and seal every penetration through both.
- Keep ducts and plumbing out of this cavity. Anything that has to cross it goes above the membrane, inside the
  insulation.
- The study and the model carry R-31.7; the drawing annotation reads R-31.6. The difference is rounding, and the
  same batt.
