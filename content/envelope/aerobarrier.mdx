title: Supplemental Air Sealing
dek: A contingency following the first airtightness test.
---

Reaching 1.0 ACH50 in an existing house is hard. The dedicated air-barrier layers described above give durable
airtightness and are the primary strategy. Some areas will turn out to be inaccessible or impractical to expose
for sealing, so keep a backup plan.

[AeroBarrier](https://aeroseal.com/industries/residential-new-construction/) seals a whole building in a day using
an aerosolized sealant that finds the remaining leaks and closes them. It works best on many small distributed gaps
and complements the membrane work; open junctions and unsealed window installations still need direct repair.
Consult an installer early so the work can be scheduled after the mid-construction blower-door test if it turns
out to be needed.
