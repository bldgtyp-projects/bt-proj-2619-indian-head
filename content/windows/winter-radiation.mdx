The winter study shows how much useful sun each window receives across the heating season, taking the climate, orientation and shading context into account. The variation across the house is large: the exposed upper windows collect useful winter sun, while windows beneath the deep overhangs and on the shaded elevations collect much less. The sunroom's glazed faces span both conditions.

In the PHPP heating balance for the As-Drawn package, the windows contribute about **2,950 kWh/yr from the south**, **2,420 from the west**, **1,960 from the east** and **640 from the north** over the heating season. That is a useful offset against heating demand, and it is small next to the heat the windows lose, which is why the low U-value across every elevation comes first.

### Recommendations

- Hold the LePage windows at an installed U-0.20 or better on every elevation. The gains above do not change  the window package; they confirm that a warm window matters more than a sunny one here.
- Given the amount of glazing on the sunroom, it may experience overheating in shoulder season (spring/fall). Ensure that all south and west facing sunroom windows are outfitted with robust user-operable blinds to help reduce the impact of unwanted solar radiation and glare.
