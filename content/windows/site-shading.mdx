Window energy balance strongly impacts how a house like this performs. The goal in this climate is to pick up winter solar gain to offset heating without buying an overheating problem in summer. The sun-path diagrams below document the building orientation and the shading context in the model: the roof overhangs, porches and adjacent wings of the house itself, the window recesses, and the trees and neighboring buildings around the site.

Those elements create very different exposure conditions around the house. Windows under deep overhangs and porches see little direct sun in either season, while the upper-story and dormer windows are exposed year round. The new sunroom has both shaded and exposed glazed faces and, with its large glass area relative to its floor area, is the room where shading and glazing choices matter most. The two radiation studies that follow put numbers on this.
