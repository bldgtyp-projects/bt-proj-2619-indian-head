The energy model uses project geometry established from the architectural references: the
**Granoff Architects** permit set of August 18, 2026 and the CAD files received on September 1, 2026. The
geometry includes the existing house, the sunroom addition, and the surrounding shading context described in the
[site shading](/windows/#site-shading) section.

All five _variants_ tested here share the same geometry: **6,587 ft² of treated floor area** (the PHPP energy-reference area; smaller than the gross floor area), **18,335 ft² of thermal envelope**, and a **60,225 ft³ airtightness test
volume**. That works out to about **2.8 ft² of envelope per ft² of floor area**. For reference, a typical compact two-story house of the same size would land closer to 2.2 to 2.5. The many roof forms, dormers, projecting bays, the garage interface and the new sunroom all add surface through which heat can esape, so insulation and air-barrier continuity at those junctions carry more weight here than on a simpler home.

The model includes about **1,355 ft² of windows**, with the largest share facing west.
