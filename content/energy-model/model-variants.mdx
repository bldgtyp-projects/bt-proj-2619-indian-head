This assessment tests the building in five _variants_. Every variant assumes electric heat pumps for heating, cooling and hot water, and every variant carries the same 0.40 SHGC glazing, so the differences below come from the envelope and ventilation inputs alone. The **As-Drawn variant** is the current recommended path; the other four are here for comparison.

1. **Code Minimum:** A benchmark which assumes the renovation work is built to only meet the prescriptive minimums of the [2022 Connecticut State Building Code](/energy_model/#energy-code) for new construction: code-level assembly R-values, windows at about U-0.27, 3.0 ACH50 airtightness, and a typical "extract-only" (bath fan) ventilation system. This _variant_ is included to give the other variants a common reference.

2. **As-Drawn (Recommended):** The renovation as shown in the current drawing set: the assembly studies in the
   [envelope](/building_envelope/) section, the owner's preferred LePage wood windows at an installed U-0.20, 1.0 ACH50 airtightness, a 20% thermal-bridge allowance, and a balanced ERV modeled at about 73% effective heat recovery. The fireplaces stay, and the crawlspace floor stays uninsulated at R-1.4. This is the package we recommend building in this case.

3. **EnerPHit By Component:** In this variant, the As-Drawn package is brought to prescriptive PHI EnerPHit By-Component level: windows at U-0.16, R-10 at the crawlspace floor, R-18 at the walls without exterior insulation, and a 15% thermal-bridge allowance on the strength of EnerPHit-level junction detailing. Note that to fully reach this level, the crawlspace floors would need to be insulated, the insulation on the north-side walls (at the property line) improved, and the fireplaces sealed off.

4. **PHI Low Energy Building:** This variant includes further envelope upgrades to meet the PHI "Low Energy Building" level of performance; U-0.16 windows, 0.8 ACH50, and 2 inches of insulation on the outdoor-air ducts.

5. **EnerPHit By Demand:** The package required to meet the full performance-based PHI-EnerPHit By-Demand limit:
   R-60 roofs, R-40 above-grade walls and garage ceiling, R-35 cellar walls, R-30 cellar floors, windows at about
   U-0.14, 0.6 ACH50, and an ERV at about 74% effective heat recovery. 

_R-values are effective whole-assembly values in hr-ft²-F/Btu. Window U-values are installed whole-window values in
Btu/hr-ft²-F, averaged across the house._
