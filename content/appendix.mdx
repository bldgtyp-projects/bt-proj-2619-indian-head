#### Open items carried into the next phase

- The LePage window U-value of 0.20 is a schematic-level assumption from a previous BLDGTYP project with LePage.
  Confirm it against LePage's product data for the sizes, glazing and frame options chosen here, and update the
  model before the window order.
- The window schedule in the drawing set and the window inputs in the model differ. The model values are the basis
  of this report; the two will be reconciled against the proposed window order.
- The summer resilience run will be repeated once shading and the final envelope package are set, and the
  deadly-days check added. The current summer run also shows a small amount of heating and cooling energy inside
  the outage window, which will be corrected in that rerun.
- Cooking and lighting energy are carried inside the general household electricity allowance and will be broken out
  before the site-energy total is used as a household budget.
- Room-by-room heating and cooling loads, dehumidification sizing, and equipment selection are by the mechanical
  designer. The peak loads in this report are whole-house PHPP figures for schematic sizing.
- No ERV has been selected. The ventilation schedule still has the sunroom, the cellar rooms and the guest bath
  unassigned, and no low-speed setting defined.
- No photovoltaic generation is in the model.

#### Units

Energy demand figures are whole-building annual totals in kWh/yr. Where a Passive House limit is written per unit
of floor area, we have multiplied it by this building's treated floor area of 6,587 ft² (612 m²) and report the
whole-building allowance. Peak loads stay in Btu/h. R-values are effective whole-assembly values in
hr-ft²-F/Btu; window U-values are installed values in Btu/hr-ft²-F.
