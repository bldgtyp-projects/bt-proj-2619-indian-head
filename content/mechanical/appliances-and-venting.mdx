#### Kitchen hood and makeup air

A range hood works when it captures the cooking fumes close to the surface. Hood width, depth and mounting height
set the capture; a bigger fan cannot make up for a hood that is too small or mounted too high. Mount the hood as
low as practical, extend it about six inches past the range on each side, and make it as deep as the range allows,
then pick the airflow to suit.

The model carries about **620 cfm of intermittent kitchen exhaust**. In a house at 1.0 ACH50 that much exhaust
needs a dedicated makeup-air path, or the hood will starve and depressurize the house against the fireplaces.
Provide a makeup-air fan interlocked with the hood, with a tight motorized damper on the outdoor connection, an
insulated duct, and a heating element to temper the incoming winter air so it does not land as a cold draft at
the cook. The diagram below shows the arrangement.

[Electro Industries](https://electromn.com/make-up-air-2/) and [Neptronic](https://www.neptronic.com/cmu/) make
self-modulating makeup-air systems with matching heaters that suit this project. The mechanical designer sizes the
heating capacity and the electrical demand at the selected airflow and the winter design condition, and confirms
the damper, airflow proving and service access with the supplier. Confirm the actual hood duty with the range
selection before that sizing, since a smaller hood shrinks the makeup-air system with it.

#### Retained fireplaces

**The fireplaces remain.** Their flues, dampers and combustion-air paths sit on the air barrier and interact with
every exhaust appliance in the house. Have a chimney professional verify the installations, coordinate any
modifications with the mechanical designer, and test the completed arrangement with the kitchen hood, dryer and
bathroom boost running together to confirm the fireplaces draw correctly. The fireplaces are the main reason the
recommendation stops at the EnerPHit targets rather than a certificate, and with this coordination they work fine
in an airtight house.

#### Other appliances

The model uses heat pumps for heating, cooling and domestic hot water. Locate the heat-pump water heater where its
cool exhaust air and its noise are welcome, or duct it. Choose the clothes dryer early: a heat-pump condensing
dryer needs no exhaust penetration and is the cleaner fit for an airtight house. Record every remaining exhaust in
the pressure and air-barrier plan.
