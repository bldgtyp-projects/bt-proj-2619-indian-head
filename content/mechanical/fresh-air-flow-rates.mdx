The room schedule and the four level plans below show the current fresh-air allocation. The ERV totals
**300 cfm supply and extract at high speed**, and about **231 cfm at medium speed**. The schedule is sized for the PHPP standard occupancy of about 3.5 people; the bedroom count and how the house will actually be used, including guests, set the final design. We recommend sending the floor plans to both [Zehnder America](https://zehnderamerica.com) and [Four Seven Five](https://475.supply/) in order to have them work up full system specifications for the home.

Before the ERV is selected, three items in the schedule need to be discussed:

- **The cellar rooms** have no assigned supply or extract yet. Depending on the anticipated usage, we may want to add some air-circulation down in that zone.
- **A low-speed setting.** The schedule assumes a 'high' and 'medium' speeds only. A 'low' (away) setting for times when the  house is lightly occupied saves energy and keeps the air from getting too dry in winter. This requires more complex and sophisticated controls however. 
- **The kitchen hood** is listed separately, as an intermittent allowance of about 620 cfm with matching makeup air. It runs only while cooking and is separate from the continuous ERV flow. See [Appliances and Venting](/mechanical/#appliances-and-venting).
