The room schedule and the four level plans below show the current fresh-air allocation. The ERV totals
**300 cfm supply and 275 cfm extract at high speed**, and about **231 / 212 cfm at medium speed**. The schedule is sized for the PHPP standard occupancy of about 3.5 people; the bedroom count and how the house will actually be used, including guests, set the final design.

Before the ERV is selected, three items in the schedule need to be discussed:

- **The sunroom and the cellar rooms** have no assigned supply or extract yet. Halls and closets can stay as transfer zones; occupied rooms and bathrooms each need a supply or an extract.
- **A low-speed setting.** The schedule assumes a 'high' and 'medium' speeds only. A 'low' (away) setting for times when the  house is lightly occupied saves energy and keeps the air from getting too dry in winter.
- **The kitchen hood** is listed separately, as an intermittent allowance of about 620 cfm with matching makeup air. It runs only while cooking and is separate from the continuous ERV flow. See [Appliances and Venting](/mechanical/#appliances-and-venting).

The plans show which rooms receive supply, transfer or extract air. Duct routing and equipment placement come with the mechanical design.
